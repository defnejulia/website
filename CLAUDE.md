# D.AGENCY Website

## Brand

This site is for **D.AGENCY** — "the new creative force in social media."
**Always follow the brand guidelines** when building or restyling any UI.

- **Full guidelines:** [`brand/BRAND_GUIDELINES.md`](brand/BRAND_GUIDELINES.md)
- **Design tokens (CSS):** [`brand/design-tokens.css`](brand/design-tokens.css)
- **Design tokens (JSON):** [`brand/tokens.json`](brand/tokens.json)
- **Logos:** `brand/logos/` (web PNGs) + `brand/logos/source/` (`.ai`/`.eps`)
- **The Spark element:** `brand/elements/spark*.svg`

### At-a-glance

- **Primary color:** Spicy Orange `#f82303`. Palette: Sandy Beige `#eeeae1` (bg),
  Azure Blue `#1a4999`, Baby Pink `#ffb2c2`, Little Red `#890033`.
- **Type:** Hanken Grotesk (Bold = headings, Regular = body), **Anton** for expressive
  display headlines, **Qwitcher Grypen** for small handwritten accents. All on Google Fonts.
- **Signature element:** "The Spark" — a 4-point sparkle. Use it for bullets, glow accents,
  and delight. Pair with the `->` arrow and bold uppercase CTA badges (NEW, BOOK NOW!).
- **Tone:** bold, confident, trend-aware. *"Bold ideas, real results – always on trend."*

When in doubt about a color, font, or asset, read `brand/BRAND_GUIDELINES.md` rather than guessing.
